# Creative brief: <brand / page>

Fill this in before designing; a design without one is a guess.

1. Deliverable, date, owner, who signs off.
2. Goal and the one metric that shows it worked.
3. Audience: who, which phone, which network, which language, where they are when they look (indoors, at the pond in bright sun).
4. Insight: the tension in their day that the product resolves.
5. Brand idea in one sentence. Test: could a stranger guess the brand from a screenshot with the logo removed?
6. Proof: 3–4 facts we may claim, each with its source. Facts we may not claim.
7. The one action we want (buy, ask on WhatsApp, request a quote); rank the rest.
8. Sections of the homepage and of the product page, screen by screen.
9. Voice: three adjectives, three "nevers", one reference.
10. References the client loves (2–3 links or screenshots) and what exactly they love in each.
11. Art direction: place, light, people, palette, the signature shot, banned shots.
12. Shot list and video list; what exists now; the fallback if the shoot never happens.
13. Type (display, text, labels) and colour (ink, paper, one owned accent).
14. Motion: the signature move, durations, easing, the reduced-motion plan.
15. Speed budget: LCP, page KiB, JavaScript KiB, the video rule.
16. Must-haves and things to avoid.
