# Selling a machine online: the hardware playbook

Distilled from research on 2026-11-20: hardware brand sites (Apple, DJI, EcoFlow, Insta360, Roborock, Dreame, Bambu Lab, with thinner reads of YETI, Fellow, Dyson, Anker SOLIX and Rivian), Chinese 独立站 course material, DTC agency playbooks, Indonesian norms and the competitive baseline. Read it when designing a home page or product page for a machine, and before writing any trust, payment or delivery copy for WANGJIA. Measured teardowns are in `teardowns.md`; UX rules in `ecommerce-ux.md`.

## 1. Ten findings

1. **Story and buying are split** at Apple, DJI, EcoFlow, Insta360 and Bambu: a long story page plus a plain buy page (price, the monthly figure right under it, bundles, what is in the box, comparison with the next model, FAQ, "ask an expert"). WANGJIA's farmers arrive from WhatsApp links straight on a product, so keep one page per machine with the buy box first and story below; the home page tells the flagship's story.
2. **The hero is short**: small product name, a claim of 2–9 words, at most one proof line, Buy plus Watch, and the machine or its effect in dramatic light.
3. **Specs are translated into the buyer's world**: DJI's farm drone says 150 L means two bags of fertiliser per load; numbers are drawn (bars, dials, thin-line icons), not listed.
4. **They open the machine up**: subsystem tabs (DJI Agras), callouts on a 3D model (Apple), cutaways (Dyson), named parts (YETI). For WANGJIA this is the most transferable device, and each part can carry its spare-part price.
5. **They compare with the buyer's current state or their own next model**, never a named competitor.
6. **After-sales is sold as part of the product**: Apple's "best place to shop" cards, DJI's dealer and repair network, EcoFlow's four-step installation, Dreame's "Buy with Confidence".
7. **High-ticket conversion is risk removal** (a 人人都是产品经理 teardown of EcoFlow): show the need, not the object; give decision support, not information; concentrate trust at the decision point.
8. **Indonesia is WhatsApp-first and card-poor**: WhatsApp reaches 92.7 % of internet users (DataReportal, Nov 2026); cards are about 5 % of e-commerce value; marketplace COD caps (Tokopedia Rp2.5 jt, Shopee Rp5 jt) sit below most WANGJIA machines.
9. **The local competition is weak**: template sites, 6-month warranties, no footage of a machine in an Indonesian pond, no full delivered cost, no instalment maths. The lever is clarity, proof and after-sales, not effects or price.
10. **Much of the brand playbook does not translate** to a farmer on a cheap phone (section 9).

## 2. The hero formula

| Page | First screen | Price in hero |
|---|---|---|
| Apple AirPods Pro 3 | claim + proof line, full-bleed film of the effect | price pill + Buy |
| Apple iPhone 18 Pro | name + "Pro further." (Indonesian: "Jauh lebih Pro."), looping film | Buy only |
| DJI Mavic 4 Pro | name + slogan, a landscape shot by the product | Buy + Watch |
| DJI Agras T100 (farm drone) | name + 5-character claim + a paragraph of farm outcomes | none: a dealer closes |
| Bambu Lab H2D | name + two-line headline, dark macro footage | Buy now + Watch |
| Insta360 X5 | name + 9-character claim, static key visual | Buy + intro video |

Copy elsewhere stays short: Apple highlight cards 10–25 words, Apple sections 60–80 words, Roborock features 15–90 words each with one big number. Multi-product homepages (EcoFlow, Dreame) are the weakest pages, all carousels and sale banners: copy the product-page grammar, not their homepages. WANGJIA sits between Apple and DJI Agras: a short claim, a proof line of catalogue facts, and a price pill next to the WhatsApp button.

## 3. How the best sites show a machine

- **Specs in the buyer's units**: a number plus a farm outcome, with a footnote marker.
- **Numbers drawn**: Roborock's bars against its own older models; DJI's thin-line icon around a number. WANGJIA: 1:14 as two gears turning at that ratio; "hemat listrik 25 %" as a bar with its footnote (compared with a worm gear, as the catalogue says).
- **Anatomy**: a pinned scroll scene where the machine comes apart; each label = part name, one fact, spare-part price ("Kipas kaki bebek · PP tahan UV · mulai Rp275.000"); the last frame: "Satu bagian rusak? Ganti satu bagian." Mock-up A2 does this in 3D.
- **Choose by situation**: Insta360 scenario tabs, Dreame "Shop by Need". WANGJIA: by pond type and size, once the owner gives the rules.
- **Help choosing**: Dreame's finder, DJI's "不知道怎么选？", Polytron's AC-capacity tool (Indonesian). WANGJIA: three inputs that open WhatsApp.
- **What is in the box**, with quantities (DJI Store), plus what the farmer must prepare.
- **Footnotes with test conditions** (Apple 25, Roborock 17): big claims become credible.
- **A film gallery the user controls** (Apple "Get the highlights": pause button, dot progress).
- **A dimension drawing** with a person for scale (Roborock).

## 4. Section order

- **Story page**: outcome → highlights → "is it worth it for me" comparison → design and anatomy → one chapter per capability (claim + number + picture) → service and proof → choose (compare, bundles) → specs and FAQ → where to buy. A sticky local nav with Buy (Apple, DJI).
- **Buy page (DJI Store)**: gallery with a counter (1/8) → title → price → "from ¥798/month × 6" right under it → returns, trade-in, warranty → bundles with prices, one tagged "everybody buys this" → "not sure?" → buttons → features → in the box → comparison with the next model (with prices and Buy) → FAQ → "ask an expert".
- **Exporter B2B page (雨果网 template)**: title → parameter list → selling points → three detail images (core detail, application scene, engineering drawing) → factory → certificates → process, packing, logistics → FAQ and contact. Chinese aerator exporters on made-in-china follow it exactly.

## 5. Proof and trust a farmer believes

In order of strength: the machine running in a named farmer's pond with the farmer speaking (with permission); the QR check shown working; the warehouse or office with address, photos and map; published spare-part prices and stock; who answers WhatsApp, with hours and typical reply time; real marketplace store links; a YouTube channel. Warranty years work as a badge (Fellow extends its warranty when the product is registered, the closest analogue to WANGJIA's QR activation). Awards, "No. 1" and user counts do not exist for WANGJIA and must not be invented.

## 6. What the 独立站 courses teach

- **SHOPLINE**: the first screen says in one plain sentence what you sell, for whom and why it is good; product pages lead with the key selling points in picture and text together, scene photos, user video, reviews with photos, delivery and returns stated plainly, an FAQ; mobile is restructured for one hand, not shrunk.
- **店匠 SHOPLAZZA**: brand value within three seconds; the homepage is not a product list; minimum: best-seller, category entries, one trust element.
- **Shopify 中文**: one main call to action per section and one in the first screen; a single-product store can treat its homepage as a mini landing page.
- **优设 / Baidu MEUX**: a marketplace is a shelf, an independent site is content; identity from the logo's shape and colour applied everywhere; vary layout rhythm below the first screen; keep creativity out of the buying path; a bottom-docked buy bar (吸底) on product pages; uniform containers and one grey ground when product photos are uneven.
- **人人都是产品经理 (EcoFlow teardown)**: see → interested → understand → trust → decide; a break at any step loses the sale.
- **Ezra Firestone (Smart Marketer, Zipify)**: high-ticket and single-product brands do best with long-form or "mini-site" pages; a sticky header with Buy; at least eight images; thumbnails instead of dots on mobile; a full-width mobile button; a customer testimonial at the top of the buy box raised revenue per visitor from $38.88 to $44.24 in his test; customer video reviews are the most valuable asset; FAQs come from real support questions.
- Reject the urgency widgets these courses and builders also sell (countdowns, "only 4 left", virtual sales counts): a farmer who sees the same "deal" twice stops believing the page.

## 7. Indonesia: channel, payment, instalments

- **WhatsApp** is the channel: 92.7 % of internet users monthly; replace email capture with a WhatsApp opt-in; send order updates there.
- **Payment mix** (Worldpay 2026, as quoted by Xendit): e-wallets 42 %, bank transfer 34 %, cash/COD 13 %, cards 5 %, BNPL 3 % of e-commerce value.
- **COD** rarely fits: Tokopedia's cap is Rp2.5 jt (check the current rule), Shopee's Rp5 jt, and kincir travel by cargo. The trust COD gives has to come from elsewhere: marketplace escrow, a deposit with the balance on arrival, a warehouse visit.
- **Instalments in the grammar people know: motorbike credit.** Show the down payment, the monthly amount × months, the total, and "simulasi, tidak mengikat". Kredivo tenors depend on the account's score (basic accounts get 3 months), so WANGJIA's own "bayar bertahap" carries most sales; Kredivo or Akulaku only as an extra.
- **KUR** for fisheries covers buying kincir and pumps (Permen KKP 73/2016); offering a proforma invoice for a KUR application is a cheap, concrete service.
- **Local brands**: Polytron runs its own store with prices, "Beli" on every product, a capacity tool and credit calculators; Eiger and Erigo show free-shipping rules, paylater/transfer/COD, store locators with hours, and a "why buy on our website" page.

## 8. The competitive baseline

- **ISW (Medan), Olimpia 1 HP**: Rp5,000,000, "Beli" goes to Tokopedia, five phone numbers but no WhatsApp link, bevel gear 14:1 with 103 rpm output, "hemat 20 % listrik" against worm gears, 6-month full-set warranty, free shipping to Sumatra, Java and Bali, a brochure PDF.
- **ALTEZ**: "Garansi Kepuasan Pelanggan", standard motor and gearbox so parts can be replaced alone; pages look about five years old.
- **Tokopedia "kincir tambak 2hp"**: 45 results; listings with one rating or nine sold; warranties of 6 months or "servis 1 tahun, parts 3 bulan"; 2 HP from about Rp5.2 jt to Rp6.8 jt.
- **Chinese exporters (made-in-china)**: US$220–240 a unit in container lots, spec tables claiming area and oxygen per hour, 12-month warranty, factory stats, CE/ISO, "Send Inquiry".
- "Bevel instead of worm saves 20 %+" appears across many listings, so WANGJIA's "hemat listrik 25 %" only becomes credible with a footnote and, later, a filmed ampere reading.
- **The gap WANGJIA can own**: footage in an Indonesian pond with a person, the full delivered cost per island, instalment maths, WhatsApp with hours, spare-part prices on the page, a visual system.

## 9. What does not translate

| Pattern | Why it fails here | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| 30,000–50,000 px pages with 14–16 videos | cheap Android, outdoor 4G, ~730 KiB budget | one living hero (scene or one short loop after first paint), one pinned anatomy chapter, other films on tap |
| Spec heroics in foreign units | not what decides a kincir | HP, kW, phase, amperes, impellers, floats, materials; outcomes in rupiah or pond terms once confirmed |
| Email capture for 5 % off | low email use | WhatsApp opt-in |
| Card BNPL, "0 % for 12 months" | cards ~5 % of value | bayar bertahap in motorbike form; KUR paperwork |
| Free returns, 30-day trials | a 60–90 kg machine by cargo | check on arrival, warranty claim on WhatsApp, spare parts |
| Award walls, "No. 1", user counts | none exist | named farmers, the warehouse address, the QR check |
| Countdowns, "up to 71 % off" | fake urgency | honest patungan tiers if the owner sets them |
| AR "view in your space" | no value for a pond machine | a scale photo with a person, a dimension drawing |
| Hiding the price behind a dealer form | buyers leave for competitors | price, delivery and instalment on every machine |
| Load-time overlays | block a small screen | nothing on load |

## 10. Product page outline for WANGJIA

Phone first (375 px); desktop puts the gallery at 7/12 and a sticky buy box at 5/12. A sticky sub-nav after the buy box: Ringkas · Bongkar · Spesifikasi · Bayar & kirim · Tanya.

1. **Buy box**: gallery with thumbnails and a counter (in a pond with a person for scale, studio front, three-quarter, gearbox, kipas, floats and frame, packed and loaded, a pond film on tap); title and type ("Kincir Air WANGJIA 2 HP", "Tipe YC-1.5A · 4 impeller · 3 pelampung"); the variant as two large buttons with their own prices ("3 Phase · Rp6.500.000", "1 Phase · Rp6.700.000") and "1 phase atau 3 phase? Cek listrik Anda"; under the price the instalment example and the delivery cost and days for the chosen island; one trust row; [Pesan via WhatsApp] full width, prefilled with model, variant and island; [Masukkan keranjang] as outline; a permitted farmer quote at the top if one exists.
2. **Sorotan**: four to six highlights, each a photo and two lines.
3. **Di tambak**: the pond film, a dawn photo, the scale photo.
4. **Bongkar**: the anatomy chapter; each part links to its spare-part page with its price.
5. **Yang Anda terima**: box contents with quantities and what to prepare (cable, panel, anchoring).
6. **Spesifikasi**: collapsible groups with units and footnotes; a dimension drawing with a person.
7. **Bandingkan**: the four machines; on a phone two columns with a model switcher.
8. **Bayar**: transfer or virtual account; bayar bertahap (deposit, months, monthly amount, total, fees, late payment); patungan tiers; KUR proforma.
9. **Kirim dan pasang**: cargo cost and days per island, packing photos, what to check on arrival, the installation film, QR activation in three steps.
10. **Garansi dan servis**: what the motor warranty covers, how to claim on WhatsApp, the spare-part list with prices.
11. **Tanya jawab**: six to eight questions from real WhatsApp chats.
12. **Lainnya**: the other machines and spare parts.

### As built (2026-11-20, night since 2026-11-24)
`apps/storefront/src/modules/products/templates/index.tsx`, `product.css`, `components/buy-box/` and `components/image-gallery/`: first built as a light paper page; the owner preferred the night of the home page, so it now uses the same tokens. The gallery opens on a night stage for the four machines (cut-out on dark water, the model in Archivo extra-condensed behind it, a low moon, the reflection; data in `src/lib/util/machines.ts`); catalogue photos, white squares, are multiplied onto a pool of moonlight so their white disappears; the related products sit on a light band where the night ends; gallery sticky on the left on desktop, 4:3 on phones so the price is on the first screen; label, title, catalogue subtitle; the price with "Belum termasuk PPN 11%" and the payment rule (deposit first, the rest before shipping) right under it; the variant as large buttons each with its own price, never pre-selected when a machine has two power supplies ("Pilih listrik dulu"), with a one-line 1 phase / 3 phase explainer; quantity; Add to cart and an order on WhatsApp (model, variant and quantity in the message); a bar that follows at the bottom once the buttons scroll away; trust list; specifications always open from the `spec:` metadata ("Angka dari katalog"), then the description. The form still posts to `/cart/add` without JavaScript and keeps the `toolname=add_to_cart` attributes for AI agents. Still to add when the owner gives the facts: delivery cost and days per island, the instalment amounts, box contents, a pond film.

## 11. Assets and facts to request (priority order)

1. Pond footage of the 2 HP at first light (vertical and horizontal), slow-motion spray (most phones film 240 fps), a photo with a farmer for scale.
2. Every part of a 2 HP and a 1 HP photographed on black under one top light, same lens and height.
3. A 15 s QR-check film and a 30–60 s installation film.
4. Shipping cost and days per island; instalment terms; WhatsApp hours and typical reply time; box contents; weights and dimensions.
5. The basis of "hemat listrik 25 %"; a sizing rule the owner will stand behind; permission for farmer names and quotes; links to real marketplace stores; confirmation of seed claims ("kaki bebek", "solid SS304, not pipe", "fits other brands with a 25 mm shaft").
